Emotion Word Survey

This survey has three parts. There are no right or wrong answers, so please answer based on how you feel.

  1. Emotion similarity rating. You will see emotion words two at a time and rate how similar their meanings are, from 0 to 10. There are 78 pairs in total.
  2. Grouping emotion words. Colour the 13 emotion words so that words you feel have similar meanings share the same colour.
  3. Matching faces and words. For each of the 13 face photos, choose the emotion word that fits best as your 1st choice and the next best as your 2nd choice. Rate your confidence in each from 1 to 5.

Please enter your Participant ID to start.

Part 1 of 3

Emotion Similarity Rating

Move the slider to rate how similar the meanings of the two emotion words are, from 0 to 10. You need to move the slider before you can go to the next pair, and you can press “Previous” to go back and change an earlier answer.

Part 2 of 3

Grouping Emotion Words

Give emotions you feel are similar in meaning the same colour. Pick a colour above, then tap a card to colour it; tap it again with the same colour to clear it. Tapping with a different colour selected switches the card to that colour. You can make as many groups as you like, of any size, and a word can be in a group on its own.

Colour

    Coloured cards will be listed here by group. You can give each group a name if you like.
